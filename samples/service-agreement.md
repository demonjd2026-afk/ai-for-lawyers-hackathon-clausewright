# Service Agreement

## 1. Parties

This Service Agreement is made on 10 November 2026 (the "Effective Date") between **Kaveri Organics Private Limited**, a company having its address at 42, 2nd Cross, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038, Karnataka, GSTIN 29AABCK1234L1Z5 (the **"Client"**) and **Pixelnest Studio**, a sole proprietorship having its address at Door 7-12, Beach Road, Visakhapatnam 530017, Andhra Pradesh, GSTIN 37ABCPT1234K1Z2, Udyam Registration No. UDYAM-AP-08-0012345 (the **"Service Provider"**).

## 2. Services

The Service Provider shall provide the following services (the "Services") with reasonable skill, care and diligence:

Design and build a bilingual (English and Kannada) e-commerce website for organic produce, including product catalogue, checkout integration and order management for customer data.

The Services are divided into the milestones in Schedule A. The Service Provider acts as an independent contractor and nothing in this Agreement creates employment, partnership or agency.

## 3. Delivery and Acceptance

The Service Provider shall notify the Client in writing when a milestone is delivered. The Client may, within ten (10) days of delivery, either accept the milestone or object in writing stating the specific respects in which the deliverable does not conform to Schedule A. If the Client does not object within ten (10) days, the milestone is deemed accepted on the day of delivery.

## 4. Fees and Payment

The Client shall pay the Service Provider a total fee of ₹6,00,000.50 (Rupees Six Lakh and Fifty Paise Only), payable milestone-wise as set out in Schedule A. Each milestone fee is payable within thirty (30) days of its acceptance or deemed acceptance. Fees are exclusive of GST, which the Client shall pay in addition at the applicable rate against a valid tax invoice. The parties treat the Services as professional services for the purpose of tax deduction at source. The Client may deduct tax at source as required by the Income-tax Act, 2025, shall deposit it within the statutory time, and shall issue the corresponding certificate. The Service Provider is a micro or small enterprise. Under section 15 of the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006, payment shall in no case be made later than forty-five (45) days from the day of acceptance or deemed acceptance, and any delay attracts compound interest with monthly rests at three times the bank rate notified by the Reserve Bank of India under section 16.

## 5. Escrow of Fees

To secure payment, the Client shall deposit the total fee into the escrow smart contract described in Schedule B within seven (7) days of the Effective Date. The escrow releases each milestone fee to the Service Provider on acceptance or deemed acceptance under the Delivery and Acceptance clause, returns it to the Client if the Service Provider waives it or fails to deliver by its due date plus the acceptance period, and holds it if the Client objects in time, until the parties settle or the Escrow Arbiter gives effect to the outcome under the Dispute Resolution clause. The escrow is a payment mechanism only. If its operation differs from this Agreement, this Agreement prevails and the parties shall make the payments needed to give effect to it. The escrow contract records the SHA-256 hash of this Agreement's text, and both parties confirm the hash on-chain before funding.

## 6. Intellectual Property

On payment of the fee for each milestone, the Service Provider assigns to the Client all rights, title and interest, including all copyright and the rights to reproduce, adapt, publish, communicate to the public, translate and commercially exploit, in the deliverables of that milestone, throughout the world, for the full term of copyright and any renewals, in all media now known or later developed. The consideration for this assignment is included in the fee. The parties agree that this assignment shall not lapse under section 19(4) of the Copyright Act, 1957 if the Client does not exercise the rights within one year. The Service Provider retains its pre-existing tools and know-how and grants the Client a perpetual, royalty-free licence to use any of them embedded in the deliverables. Until payment for a milestone, its deliverables remain the property of the Service Provider. The author's moral rights under section 57 of the Copyright Act, 1957 are not affected, and the Service Provider agrees not to assert them to prevent reasonable modification of the deliverables by the Client.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all non-public business, technical and financial information received from the other party in connection with this Agreement, use it only to perform this Agreement, and disclose it only to its personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations. These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public without breach, was lawfully known to the recipient before disclosure, is independently developed, or is received from a third party without restriction. A party may disclose information when required by law, court or regulator, after giving the other party prompt notice where lawful. These obligations survive for three (3) years after this Agreement ends, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 8. Personal Data

Where the Service Provider processes digital personal data on behalf of the Client, the Client acts as Data Fiduciary and the Service Provider as Data Processor under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it. The Service Provider shall: (a) process such data only on the Client's documented instructions and only for the purpose of this Agreement; (b) maintain reasonable security safeguards, including encryption or masking where appropriate, access controls, and logs of access kept for at least one (1) year; (c) notify the Client of any personal data breach within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it, with the details the Client needs to report to the Data Protection Board within its statutory time; (d) not engage a sub-processor without the Client's prior written consent and on terms no less protective; (e) assist the Client in responding to Data Principal requests and in any audit; and (f) on completion, termination or instruction, erase or return the data unless retention is required by law, and confirm erasure in writing.

## 9. Non-Solicitation of Personnel

During the term and for twelve (12) months after it ends, neither party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other party with whom it worked under this Agreement. General advertisements not targeted at such employees are not solicitation. The parties acknowledge that this clause protects confidential information and working relationships and does not restrain any person from exercising a lawful profession, trade or business.

## 10. Liability

Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the amount of the total fee, except for unpaid fees, breach of confidentiality, infringement of intellectual property, fraud and liability that cannot be limited by law. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

## 11. Term and Termination

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until all milestones are accepted and paid. Either party may terminate it for convenience on thirty (30) days written notice, or for material breach not cured within fifteen (15) days of written notice. On termination the Client shall pay for milestones accepted and, pro rata, for work performed on the current milestone up to the termination date, and each party shall return the other's confidential information.

## 12. Dispute Resolution

The parties shall first try in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement through discussions between senior representatives for fifteen (15) days from a written notice of dispute. Any dispute not so resolved shall be referred to final and binding arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 before a sole arbitrator appointed by mutual written consent of the parties or, failing agreement within thirty (30) days, by the court under section 11 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Bengaluru, and the language shall be English. Neither party shall unilaterally appoint the arbitrator. Nothing in this clause restricts the Service Provider's right, as a micro or small enterprise, to refer a dispute regarding amounts due to the Micro and Small Enterprises Facilitation Council having jurisdiction where the Service Provider is located, under section 18 of the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006, and that right prevails over the seat stated above.

## 13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Subject to the dispute resolution clause, the courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 14. Stamp Duty

The Client shall pay the stamp duty payable on this Agreement under the stamp law applicable in Karnataka, including through e-stamping, before or at the time of execution.

## 15. General

This Agreement, with its Schedules, is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject and supersedes earlier understandings. It may be amended only in writing signed by both parties. Neither party may assign it without the other's written consent, except to a successor of its whole business. If any provision is held invalid, the rest continues in force and the invalid provision is replaced by a valid one closest to its intent. Notices shall be in writing to the email addresses in this Agreement. Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other promptly and resumes performance as soon as practicable.
