# Letter of Appointment

## 1. Appointment

**Pixelnest Studio**, a sole proprietorship having its address at Door 7-12, Beach Road, Visakhapatnam 530017, Andhra Pradesh (the **"Employer"**) appoints **Sravani Kota**, an individual residing at 12-4-56, MVP Colony, Visakhapatnam 530017, Andhra Pradesh (the **"Employee"**) on the terms of this letter, issued on 10 November 2026 under section 6(1)(f) of the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020.

## 2. Designation and Duties

The Employee is appointed as UI Designer in the skilled category, to work at Visakhapatnam studio, with effect from 1 December 2026. The Employee's duties are:

Design web and mobile interfaces for client projects and maintain the design system.

## 3. Nature of Employment

This is a fixed-term employment from 1 December 2026 to 30 November 2027, ending automatically on that date without notice or retrenchment compensation. The Employee shall have the same hours of work, wages, allowances and other benefits as a permanent employee doing the same or similar work, and shall receive gratuity in proportion to service if the Employee completes one (1) year of continuous service. The first three (3) months are a probation period, during which either party may end the employment on seven (7) days written notice.

## 4. Working Hours

Normal working hours are 48 hours a week, within the daily limits and rest intervals under the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020. Overtime is voluntary and is paid at twice the ordinary rate of wages.

## 5. Remuneration

The Employer shall pay the Employee monthly, by bank transfer, no later than the seventh (7th) day of the following month:

| Component | Monthly | Monthly in words |
|---|---|---|
| Basic pay | ₹18,000.00 | Rupees Eighteen Thousand Only |
| House rent allowance | ₹9,000.00 | Rupees Nine Thousand Only |
| Conveyance allowance | ₹2,000.00 | Rupees Two Thousand Only |
| Other allowances | ₹11,000.00 | Rupees Eleven Thousand Only |
| **Gross monthly remuneration** | ₹40,000.00 | Rupees Forty Thousand Only |
| **Annual gross remuneration** | ₹4,80,000.00 | Rupees Four Lakh Eighty Thousand Only |

For the Code on Wages, 2019, wages are ₹20,000.00 a month, including allowances above half of total remuneration that the Code treats as wages. Tax is deducted at source as required by law.

## 6. Statutory Benefits

The Employee is covered by the Employees' Provident Fund on the statutory wage base. The Employees' State Insurance scheme does not apply to the Employee's wages. Gratuity is payable under the Code on Social Security, 2020, pro rata after one (1) year of continuous service. Leave, holidays and maternity benefit are as provided by law and the Employer's leave policy.

## 7. Confidentiality and Work Product

The Employee shall keep the Employer's confidential information and trade secrets confidential during and after employment and use them only for the Employer's business. Copyright in work made by the Employee in the course of employment belongs to the Employer under section 17 of the Copyright Act, 1957, and the Employee shall sign any document reasonably needed to record it.

## 8. Non-Solicitation of Staff

For six (6) months after leaving, the Employee shall not induce any employee of the Employer with whom the Employee worked to leave the Employer. This does not restrict the Employee from taking up any employment, profession, trade or business.

## 9. Employee Personal Data

The Employer processes the Employee's personal data for employment, payroll, statutory compliance and security, under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and keeps it only as long as needed for those purposes or as required by law. Aadhaar details are collected only with the Employee's consent.

## 10. Resignation, Termination and Final Settlement

After probation, the Employee may resign on thirty (30) days written notice, or pay in lieu. The Employer may end the employment only in accordance with law, including notice or pay in lieu and retrenchment compensation under the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 where applicable. All wages due on resignation, removal, retrenchment or closure shall be paid within two (2) working days, and gratuity within the time required by law.

## 11. Disputes and Governing Law

This letter is governed by the laws of India. Disputes are subject to the grievance redress and dispute resolution machinery under the labour codes where it applies, and otherwise to the courts at Visakhapatnam.
