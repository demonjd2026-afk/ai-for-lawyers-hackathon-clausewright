# Review of a contract received from a client

Rules and AI review. The AI reviewer may cite only authorities retrieved from the verified list for that clause; 1 citation(s) it produced outside that list were dropped.

## Clause 2: high risk (AI reviewer)

> 2. Fees. The Company shall pay the Consultant within ninety (90) days of receipt of a valid invoice. Payment for any phase is subject to receipt of payment from its client for the corresponding phase.

A 90-day payment term plus a "pay when paid" condition means you may wait a long time, or never be paid, if the Company's client defaults. If you are a registered micro or small enterprise, the law caps payment at 45 days from acceptance regardless of what the contract says.

**Ask for:** Pay within 30 days (no more than 45 days) of the invoice. Delete the condition that payment depends on the Company's client paying it.

Authorities: MSMED Act, 2006 s.15; MSMED (Amendment) Act, 2026 Act No. 16 of 2026

## Clause 3: high risk (AI reviewer)

> 3. Delay. Time is of the essence. A penalty of 2% of the total fee per day of delay shall be deducted from any amount due.

A 2% per day penalty can wipe out your whole fee in 50 days. It also applies even when the Company itself caused the delay. Courts award only reasonable compensation up to the stated sum, but the Company can simply deduct the amount from your payment first.

**Ask for:** Replace it with capped liquidated damages, for example 0.5% per week up to 10% of the fee. Apply them only to delays caused by you, and extend timelines for delays caused by the Company.

Authorities: Indian Contract Act, 1872 s.74

## Clause 6: high risk (fixed rule)

> 6. Restriction. The Consultant shall not, for two years after termination of this Agreement, provide services to any business similar to the Company's.

Post-term non-compete: void under section 27 except on sale of goodwill.

**Ask for:** Delete, or limit to the term of the agreement and rely on confidentiality.

Authorities: Indian Contract Act, 1872 s.27; Percept D'Mark v Zaheer Khan (2006) Supreme Court

## Clause 7: high risk (fixed rule)

> 7. Liability. The Consultant shall indemnify the Company against any and all losses, howsoever arising.

Uncapped liability or a one-sided indemnity for all losses.

**Ask for:** Cap liability at the contract value and make indemnities mutual and fault-based.

Authorities: Indian Contract Act, 1872 s.73

## Clause 8: high risk (fixed rule)

> 8. Claims. Any claim by the Consultant shall be barred and deemed waived unless made in writing within 15 days of the event giving rise to it.

Clause extinguishes claims after a short period, which is void under section 28.

**Ask for:** Convert to a notice requirement that does not bar the right to sue.

Authorities: Indian Contract Act, 1872 s.28; Limitation Act, 1963 Schedule, Art. 55

## Clause 8: high risk (AI reviewer)

> 8. Claims. Any claim by the Consultant shall be barred and deemed waived unless made in writing within 15 days of the event giving rise to it.

Waiving your claims unless you raise them within 15 days likely conflicts with Indian contract law, which voids clauses that extinguish rights after a period. It still risks you losing claims, including unpaid fees.

**Ask for:** Delete the clause, or make it mutual with a reasonable notice period that does not waive rights under the law of limitation.

Authorities: none

## Clause 9: high risk (fixed rule)

> 9. Disputes. Any dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by the Company. The venue shall be Mumbai.

One party appoints the arbitrator alone, which the Supreme Court has held invalid.

**Ask for:** Appointment by mutual consent, by a named institution, or by the court under section 11.

Authorities: CORE v ECI SPIC SMO MCML (JV) 2024 INSC 857 (Constitution Bench, 8 Nov 2024); Perkins Eastman Architects v HSCC (2019) Supreme Court

## Clause 9: high risk (AI reviewer)

> 9. Disputes. Any dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by the Company. The venue shall be Mumbai.

Letting the Company alone appoint the sole arbitrator is not permitted, because a party with an interest cannot unilaterally choose the arbitrator. A Mumbai venue may also be inconvenient and costly for you.

**Ask for:** Appoint the sole arbitrator by mutual agreement, failing which under the Arbitration Act through the court. Choose a venue convenient to you. Preserve your right, as an MSE, to approach the MSME Facilitation Council over unpaid dues.

Authorities: Perkins Eastman Architects v HSCC (2019) Supreme Court; CORE v ECI SPIC SMO MCML (JV) 2024 INSC 857 (Constitution Bench, 8 Nov 2024); MSMED Act, 2006 s.18

## Clause 1: medium risk (AI reviewer)

> 1. Services. The Consultant shall develop a customer loyalty app for the Company as directed from time to time.

The scope "as directed from time to time" is open-ended, so the Company can keep adding work without paying more.

**Ask for:** Attach a defined scope of work with deliverables and milestones, and state that any extra work needs a written change order and additional fees.

Authorities: none

## Clause 2: medium risk (fixed rule)

> 2. Fees. The Company shall pay the Consultant within ninety (90) days of receipt of a valid invoice. Payment for any phase is subject to receipt of payment from its client for the corresponding phase.

Payment term longer than 45 days; void against a micro or small supplier and costly for its buyer.

**Ask for:** If you are an MSE, insist on 45 days or less from acceptance and cite section 15.

Authorities: MSMED Act, 2006 s.15; Income-tax Act, 2025 s.37(2)(g) (formerly s.43B(h), 1961 Act)

## Clause 2: medium risk (fixed rule)

> 2. Fees. The Company shall pay the Consultant within ninety (90) days of receipt of a valid invoice. Payment for any phase is subject to receipt of payment from its client for the corresponding phase.

Pay-when-paid: your payment depends on a third party paying the client.

**Ask for:** Tie payment to acceptance of your deliverable, not to the client's own receivables.

Authorities: MSMED Act, 2006 s.15

## Clause 3: medium risk (fixed rule)

> 3. Delay. Time is of the essence. A penalty of 2% of the total fee per day of delay shall be deducted from any amount due.

Penalty or forfeiture: only reasonable compensation up to the stated sum is recoverable.

**Ask for:** Frame as a capped genuine pre-estimate of loss.

Authorities: Indian Contract Act, 1872 s.74; Kailash Nath Associates v DDA (2015) 4 SCC 136

## Clause 4: medium risk (AI reviewer)

> 4. Intellectual Property. The Consultant hereby assigns all intellectual property in the work to the Company.

You assign all IP immediately, before you are paid. The assignment could also sweep in your pre-existing tools and code.

**Ask for:** Make the assignment effective only on full payment. Exclude your pre-existing materials and give the Company a licence to use them. Specify the rights, territory and duration of the assignment.

Authorities: none

## Clause 5: medium risk (fixed rule)

> 5. Data. The Consultant will process customer data of the Company's members to send loyalty offers.

Handles personal data without processor obligations.

**Ask for:** Add DPDP processor terms: instructions, safeguards, breach notice, deletion.

Authorities: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 s.8

## Clause 5: medium risk (AI reviewer)

> 5. Data. The Consultant will process customer data of the Company's members to send loyalty offers.

You will handle customer personal data with no written instructions, security terms or allocation of responsibility. That exposes you to liability for breaches.

**Ask for:** Add a data processing clause. It should state that the Company is the Data Fiduciary, that you act only on its documented instructions, the security measures required, breach notification steps, and deletion or return of data at the end.

Authorities: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 s.8; Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 Rules 6-8 (notified 13 Nov 2025)

## Clause 6: medium risk (AI reviewer)

> 6. Restriction. The Consultant shall not, for two years after termination of this Agreement, provide services to any business similar to the Company's.

A two-year ban on working for similar businesses after termination is generally void in India. However, it could still be used to pressure you or start litigation.

**Ask for:** Delete it. If needed, limit it to non-solicitation of the Company's named clients during the term, plus confidentiality obligations.

Authorities: Indian Contract Act, 1872 s.27

## Clause 4: low risk (fixed rule)

> 4. Intellectual Property. The Consultant hereby assigns all intellectual property in the work to the Company.

IP assignment that does not state territory; the law then presumes India only.

**Ask for:** State the territory (worldwide) and duration expressly.

Authorities: Copyright Act, 1957 s.19
