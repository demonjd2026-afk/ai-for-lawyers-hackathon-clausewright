# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties

This Agreement is made on 10 November 2026 between **Kaveri Organics Private Limited**, a company having its address at 42, 2nd Cross, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038, Karnataka (the **"Party A"**) and **Meera Iyer**, an individual residing at Flat 3B, Adyar, Chennai 600020, Tamil Nadu (the **"Party B"**).

## 2. Purpose

The parties wish to exchange confidential information solely for the following purpose (the "Purpose"):

Evaluating a possible partnership to supply cold-pressed oils to Kaveri's retail stores.

## 3. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed in any form in connection with the Purpose, whether or not marked confidential, that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including business plans, customer data, pricing, source code, designs and know-how. It excludes information that is or becomes public without breach, was lawfully known before disclosure, is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information, or is lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

## 4. Obligations

Each party shall use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose; protect it with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information and no less than reasonable care; disclose it only to its personnel and advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by equivalent obligations; and promptly notify the other party of any unauthorised use or disclosure. Disclosure required by law, court or regulator is permitted after prompt notice where lawful, limited to what is required.

## 5. Term and Return

The obligations last for three (3) years from the Effective Date, and indefinitely for trade secrets and personal data. On request or at the end of the Purpose, the recipient shall return or destroy the Confidential Information and confirm this in writing, except copies retained under law or automatic backups, which remain confidential.

## 6. No Licence or Obligation

No licence to any intellectual property is granted, and neither party is obliged to enter into any further transaction. Information is provided "as is".

## 7. Remedies

Unauthorised disclosure may cause harm for which damages are not an adequate remedy, and the disclosing party may seek an injunction under the Specific Relief Act, 1963 in addition to any other remedy.

## 8. Dispute Resolution

The parties shall first try in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement through discussions between senior representatives for fifteen (15) days from a written notice of dispute. Any dispute not so resolved shall be referred to final and binding arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 before a sole arbitrator appointed by mutual written consent of the parties or, failing agreement within thirty (30) days, by the court under section 11 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Chennai, and the language shall be English. Neither party shall unilaterally appoint the arbitrator.

## 9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Subject to the dispute resolution clause, the courts at Chennai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 10. Stamp Duty

The Client shall pay the stamp duty payable on this Agreement under the stamp law applicable in Tamil Nadu, including through e-stamping, before or at the time of execution.

## 11. General

This Agreement, with its Schedules, is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject and supersedes earlier understandings. It may be amended only in writing signed by both parties. Neither party may assign it without the other's written consent, except to a successor of its whole business. If any provision is held invalid, the rest continues in force and the invalid provision is replaced by a valid one closest to its intent. Notices shall be in writing to the email addresses in this Agreement. Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other promptly and resumes performance as soon as practicable.
