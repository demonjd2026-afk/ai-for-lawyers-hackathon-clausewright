# Review of a contract received from a client

Rule layer only (no AI key used).

## Clause 6: high risk (fixed rule)

> 6. Restriction. The Consultant shall not, for two years after termination of this Agreement, provide services to any business similar to the Company's.

Post-term non-compete: void under section 27 except on sale of goodwill.

**Ask for:** Delete, or limit to the term of the agreement and rely on confidentiality.

Authorities: Indian Contract Act, 1872 s.27; Percept D'Mark v Zaheer Khan (2006) Supreme Court

## Clause 7: high risk (fixed rule)

> 7. Liability. The Consultant shall indemnify the Company against any and all losses, howsoever arising.

Uncapped liability or a one-sided indemnity for all losses.

**Ask for:** Cap liability at the contract value and make indemnities mutual and fault-based.

Authorities: Indian Contract Act, 1872 s.73

## Clause 8: high risk (fixed rule)

> 8. Claims. Any claim by the Consultant shall be barred and deemed waived unless made in writing within 15 days of the event giving rise to it.

Clause extinguishes claims after a short period, which is void under section 28.

**Ask for:** Convert to a notice requirement that does not bar the right to sue.

Authorities: Indian Contract Act, 1872 s.28; Limitation Act, 1963 Schedule, Art. 55

## Clause 9: high risk (fixed rule)

> 9. Disputes. Any dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by the Company. The venue shall be Mumbai.

One party appoints the arbitrator alone, which the Supreme Court has held invalid.

**Ask for:** Appointment by mutual consent, by a named institution, or by the court under section 11.

Authorities: CORE v ECI SPIC SMO MCML (JV) 2024 INSC 857 (Constitution Bench, 8 Nov 2024); Perkins Eastman Architects v HSCC (2019) Supreme Court

## Clause 2: medium risk (fixed rule)

> 2. Fees. The Company shall pay the Consultant within ninety (90) days of receipt of a valid invoice. Payment for any phase is subject to receipt of payment from its client for the corresponding phase.

Payment term longer than 45 days; void against a micro or small supplier and costly for its buyer.

**Ask for:** If you are an MSE, insist on 45 days or less from acceptance and cite section 15.

Authorities: MSMED Act, 2006 s.15; Income-tax Act, 2025 s.37(2)(g) (formerly s.43B(h), 1961 Act)

## Clause 2: medium risk (fixed rule)

> 2. Fees. The Company shall pay the Consultant within ninety (90) days of receipt of a valid invoice. Payment for any phase is subject to receipt of payment from its client for the corresponding phase.

Pay-when-paid: your payment depends on a third party paying the client.

**Ask for:** Tie payment to acceptance of your deliverable, not to the client's own receivables.

Authorities: MSMED Act, 2006 s.15

## Clause 3: medium risk (fixed rule)

> 3. Delay. Time is of the essence. A penalty of 2% of the total fee per day of delay shall be deducted from any amount due.

Penalty or forfeiture: only reasonable compensation up to the stated sum is recoverable.

**Ask for:** Frame as a capped genuine pre-estimate of loss.

Authorities: Indian Contract Act, 1872 s.74; Kailash Nath Associates v DDA (2015) 4 SCC 136

## Clause 5: medium risk (fixed rule)

> 5. Data. The Consultant will process customer data of the Company's members to send loyalty offers.

Handles personal data without processor obligations.

**Ask for:** Add DPDP processor terms: instructions, safeguards, breach notice, deletion.

Authorities: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 s.8

## Clause 4: low risk (fixed rule)

> 4. Intellectual Property. The Consultant hereby assigns all intellectual property in the work to the Company.

IP assignment that does not state territory; the law then presumes India only.

**Ask for:** State the territory (worldwide) and duration expressly.

Authorities: Copyright Act, 1957 s.19
