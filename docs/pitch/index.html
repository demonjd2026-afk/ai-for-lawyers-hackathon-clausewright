The problem

Small suppliers sign what they can't check, then wait to be paid.

₹20,979 crin delayed payments to micro and small enterprises, still pending before MSME Samadhaan on 14 Aug 2026.
  • 1Clauses courts won't enforce. Post-term non-competes, a one-sided arbitrator, penalty clauses: all common in templates.
  • 2Payment terms the law forbids. The MSMED Act caps credit at 45 days. Contracts still say 60 or 90.
  • 3AI that invents its sources. General chat tools draft fluently and cite cases that don't exist.
The idea

One term sheet. Two outputs. Proven identical.

  • AA contract drafted from fixed rules, where every check cites the provision or judgment it comes from.
  • BAn escrow that holds the fee and releases it milestone by milestone, with settings generated from the same terms.
  • =A verifier that reads the escrow back from the chain and compares it with the words on the page.
How it works

Five steps, one source of truth.

  1. DescribePlain English or Hinglish. AI extracts the terms, and each value must be backed by the user's own words.
  2. Check12 rule families test the terms against Indian law. Every finding carries its authority and a one-click fix.
  3. DraftClauses are generated the same way every time, then fingerprinted with SHA-256.
  4. BindThe escrow's settings are printed inside the contract as Schedule B and deployed.
  5. VerifyText, schedule and chain are compared in 63 separate checks.
What's new

The contract and its code cannot drift apart.

  • 63A three-way check. 46 checks tie the text to Schedule B; 17 more are read back from the chain. One mismatch fails the seal.
  • AIAI on a short leash. The reviewer may cite only from a verified list of authorities. Anything else is dropped before you see it.
  • #A seal you can read. The rosette is engraved from the document's hash. Change one character and the picture changes.
